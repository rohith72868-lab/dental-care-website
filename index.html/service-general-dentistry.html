Proactive Oral Care That Protects Natural Teeth

Preventative dentistry is the cornerstone of lifelong wellness. Our gentle ultrasonic cleanings remove stubborn tartar (calculus) and bio-film that daily toothbrushing cannot reach, keeping your gums firm, pink, and free of periodontal inflammation.

Every visit includes a comprehensive oral cancer screening, low-dose digital imaging, and customized home hygiene guidance.

When Should You Schedule a Visit?

  • • It has been more than 6 months since your last professional cleaning.
  • • Gums bleed when brushing or flossing.
  • • Sensitivity to hot tea, cold ice water, or sweet foods.
  • • Rough spots, chips, or dark spots visible on tooth surfaces.
