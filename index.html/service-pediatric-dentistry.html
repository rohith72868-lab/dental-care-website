A Pediatric Experience Kids Actually Look Forward To

Dr. Sarah Jenkins and our pediatric nursing team speak "kid language" using tell-show-do techniques. Before touching any instrument, we explain everything in playful terms, letting your child touch the water sprayer or count their teeth with the magic mirror.

Our pediatric suite features interactive games, ceiling cartoons, kid-sized sunglasses, and our beloved treasure chest prize box!

Our Pediatric Services Include:

  • • First Dental Birthday Visits: Early oral exam & parent tips.
  • • Protective Dental Sealants: Shields deep molar grooves against cavities.
  • • Fluoride Therapy: Fortifies developing baby & adult enamel.
  • • Early Orthodontic Guidance: Monitors palate growth and space.
