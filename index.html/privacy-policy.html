Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At BrightBite Dental Clinic, patient trust and confidentiality are paramount. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, safeguard, and utilize personal and medical data when you visit our website or book an appointment.

1. Health Data & HIPAA Compliance

All appointment requests, health symptoms, and personal contact details submitted through our platform are protected in accordance with HIPAA standards and state healthcare privacy laws. We do not sell, rent, or lease patient records to any third parties.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including full name, telephone number, email address, preferred appointment time, dental concerns, and insurance details for verification.

3. Communication Preferences

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive appointment reminder texts and WhatsApp confirmations from our clinic desk. You may opt out of automated notifications at any time.

4. Contact Us Regarding Your Privacy

If you have any questions regarding your medical data or wish to request copies of your dental records, please email our privacy officer at privacy@brightbitedental.com.
