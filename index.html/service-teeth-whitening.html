Why Choose In-Office Whitening?

Over-the-counter strips and whitening toothpastes only scrub surface stains and frequently cause painful enamel erosion or sharp zings of sensitivity.

Our in-clinic Phillips Zoom! treatment is supervised by certified dental professionals. We carefully protect your sensitive gum tissue with a barrier resin before applying medical-strength hydrogen peroxide activated by variable-intensity blue LED light.

Common Causes of Tooth Discoloration:

  • • Daily Coffee & Tea: Chromogens stick to tooth enamel over time.
  • • Red Wine & Dark Foods: Acids weaken enamel, letting dark tannins penetrate.
  • • Natural Aging: Outer enamel thins, revealing yellowish inner dentin.
  • • Special Events: Preparing for weddings, reunions, or media appearances.
