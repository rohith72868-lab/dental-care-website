What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a biocompatible titanium fixture surgically placed into the jawbone beneath your gums. Once healed, it acts as a permanent artificial root onto which a handcrafted zirconia porcelain crown is secured.

Unlike traditional bridges which require grinding down healthy neighboring teeth, or removable dentures that slip and irritate gums, dental implants preserve your natural jawbone and feel 100% like real teeth.

Are You Experiencing Any of These Issues?

  • • Missing one or more teeth due to decay, trauma, or gum issues.
  • • Difficulty chewing your favorite foods like steak, nuts, or apples.
  • • Slipping, loose, or uncomfortable removable dentures.
  • • Sunken facial appearance or bone loss caused by tooth gaps.

Who Is It Suitable For?

Dental implants are ideal for virtually any adult in good general health with missing teeth. You are an excellent candidate if you:

  • ✓ Have one or multiple missing teeth
  • ✓ Have healthy gums and adequate jawbone density (or qualify for gentle bone grafting)
  • ✓ Are seeking a permanent non-removable solution
  • ✓ Want to preserve facial youthfulness and prevent adjacent teeth from shifting
